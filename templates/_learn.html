What is customer segmentation?

It means sorting your customers into groups that buy in a similar way, so you can talk to each group differently.

How we group them

  • Recency: how long ago they last bought
  • Frequency: how often they buy
  • Money: how much they spend

Why shops use it

  • Reward your best customers so they stay
  • Win back customers who stopped buying
  • Spend less on ads that do not work

What you get here

  • Customer groups and who to target first
  • Fast, slow and low-stock products
  • Demand forecast and best selling time
